# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Gym users, gym owners, and health-conscious individuals. They are likely using this on the go (mobile first) or managing the gym (owners). 

## Product Purpose
A tech-forward, functional gym product interface that provides tracking, dashboarding, and health insights.

## Brand Personality
Tech, Health, Functionality. It should feel like a premium, performance-oriented tool rather than a generic fitness template.

## Anti-references
Avoid cliché fitness tropes (generic "sweaty" stock photos, aggressive neon colors, or chaotic layouts). 

## Design Principles
- Performance over fluff: the interface should be highly functional and responsive.
- Clarity in data: metrics should be instantly readable.
- Apple-grade precision: smooth interactions and high-quality craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance. High contrast for readability in gym lighting conditions (bright overheads or dim mood lighting).
